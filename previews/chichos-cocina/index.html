GOOD FOOD HAS ROOTS

It starts
with family.

Before Chicho’s, there was a family restaurant, a young cook, and a father who inspired it all.

Story adapted from the family’s official account at sister restaurant Masa Taco Bar.

1995

A Stamford beginning

Robert Monegro’s parents opened Flamboyant, the family restaurant where he grew up and found his love of cooking.

2020

A name close to home

Robert opened Chicho’s Cocina, named for his father, “Chicho.” A family story found a new home on Shippan Avenue.

TODAY

A neighborhood cocina

A small takeout taqueria, a family’s continuing story, and Mexican flavors here in Stamford.
