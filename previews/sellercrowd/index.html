A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Who is SellerCrowd for?+

Media sellers working with national advertisers and agencies—from streaming and digital media to ad tech, podcasting, and out-of-home.

Where does the intelligence come from?+

SellerCrowd’s community shares first-hand knowledge about buyers, agency teams, and account changes. Contributions are reviewed by its research team to keep the information useful and relevant.

What can I learn about a prospect?+

Explore brand and agency org charts, media responsibilities, team relationships, and insights from fellow sellers. Follow prospects to stay informed as new information is shared.

How do I get started?+
