A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

Getting started shouldn’t be the hard part.

What can I practice with Quippy?+

Everyday conversations, from saying hello and making plans to reconnecting with someone or sharing how you really feel. Quippy creates scenarios and drills around your goals.

Do I have to talk out loud?+

You can practice by voice or text. Choose the way that feels comfortable, then build from there.

How much time should I set aside?+

Quippy is designed around about ten minutes of daily practice. Small, repeatable sessions make it easier to keep showing up.

Is this the real Quippy app?+

This website is an independent design concept. The conversation preview uses illustrative examples. The App Store and Google Play buttons take you to Quippy’s official download links.
