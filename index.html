I’m AJ, a senior product designer at MartianCraft and an independent app founder based in Fier, Albania.

I like the whole process: understanding the problem, finding the simplest interaction, obsessing over the details, and getting it into people’s hands.

At MartianCraft, I design native iPhone and iPad experiences with teams of developers and project managers. In my own products, I connect that design work with SwiftUI, launch, and growth.

A LITTLE OFF-SCREEN

Former NCAA basketball captain and 1,000-point scorer. I also played overseas in Albania for two years, averaging 22 points per game. Still bringing the same teamwork and persistence to everything I make.
